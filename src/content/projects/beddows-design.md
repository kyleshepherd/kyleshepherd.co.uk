A freelance project for Alex Beddows, a multi-disciplinary artist working across 3D game art and photography. He needed a portfolio site that could hold both disciplines without either one feeling like an afterthought.

The site is built with SvelteKit and Sanity, so Alex controls his own content and can lay out each project however he wants it.
