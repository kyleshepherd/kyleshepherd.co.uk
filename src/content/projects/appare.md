A freelance project for Appare Yosakoi, a Vancouver-based dance group. They needed a website to showcase their performances and give events and organisations a way to enquire about bookings.

The site is built with SvelteKit and uses Sanity as a CMS, so the team manage the content themselves. They can add performances, swap out gallery images and edit text across the site without needing a developer.
