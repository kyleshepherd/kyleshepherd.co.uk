Reeve is a facilities management platform, built as a startup inside Kitt. It handles a service request from the moment it lands through to sign-off: triage, quoting, supplier coordination, dispatching an operative, and collecting the compliance evidence at the end. The customers are property teams looking after several buildings at once.

I started on Reeve in May 2026 as one of three founding product engineers, so the work runs from deciding what to build through to shipping it. The API is NestJS and Fastify serving GraphQL over Postgres, the web app is TanStack Start, and Resend handles transactional email.

I also work on Operative, the React Native app operatives use out on site. They pick up jobs on it, photograph what they've done and close out the visit, all against the same backend. Both apps are still in active development.
